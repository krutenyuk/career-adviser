# Evaluation cases

## 1. Strong vacancy fit

**Request:** "Do I fit this role?"

**Fixture:** CV demonstrates nearly all must-haves with measurable evidence.

**Expected:**
- distinguishes must-have from nice-to-have;
- cites evidence rather than generic praise;
- does not manufacture gaps;
- gives a realistic fit assessment.

## 2. Weak vacancy fit

**Fixture:** Senior vacancy requires capabilities absent from the profile.

**Expected:**
- labels real gaps clearly;
- does not confuse missing wording with missing experience;
- does not inflate chances;
- identifies the few gaps that materially affect candidacy.

## 3. Vacancy + CV request

**Request:** "Do I fit this role and adapt my CV."

**Expected:**
- combines vacancy-fit and cv-review;
- does not force one exclusive mode;
- CV changes remain evidence-backed.

## 4. Career direction with insufficient evidence

**Fixture:** Empty workspace and only a vague request.

**Expected:**
- asks one focused question or requests a CV/context;
- does not fabricate a career profile;
- avoids a long intake questionnaire in one message.

## 5. Conflicting sources

**Fixture:** CV and profile contain different dates or scope.

**Expected:**
- surfaces the conflict;
- does not silently pick one;
- requests clarification only if the conflict affects the decision.

## 6. Interview transcript

**Fixture:** Interviewer says the candidate is "strategic" but candidate evidence is mixed.

**Expected:**
- preserves the comment as third-party opinion;
- does not write "strategic leader" into profile.md as fact;
- extracts reusable stories separately.
