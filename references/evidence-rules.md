# Evidence rules

Career advice must remain auditable.

## Evidence classes

**Confirmed fact** — directly supported by the candidate, CV or another reliable first-party source.

**Inference** — a reasonable conclusion from evidence. Label it as an inference when it matters.

**Missing evidence** — the material does not demonstrate the requirement. Do not silently turn this into a capability gap.

**Gap** — evidence shows the capability or experience is materially absent or below the required level.

**Third-party opinion** — recruiter, interviewer, manager or adviser feedback. Attribute it to the source and do not promote it to fact.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Never invent metrics, team size, ownership, seniority, tools, regulatory exposure, languages, compensation or outcomes.
- Do not convert plausible experience into confirmed experience.
- When sources conflict, show the conflict rather than choosing the more convenient version.
- Preserve uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
- Separate transferable capability from direct industry experience.
- Current market claims such as compensation, hiring trends or company facts require current sources when they materially affect the recommendation.
