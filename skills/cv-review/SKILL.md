# CV Review

Read the relevant CV and, when useful, the target vacancy, `profile.md` and prior evidence.

Review:
- target positioning and seniority signal;
- headline and summary;
- scope, ownership and measurable impact;
- chronology, clarity and readability;
- role- and region-specific terminology;
- missing evidence and unsupported claims;
- keyword coverage without keyword stuffing;
- language quality and consistency.

Prioritise the highest-impact changes. Give concrete replacement wording when useful.

Never invent achievements, numbers, team sizes, tools or responsibilities. Use vacancy language only where candidate evidence supports it.

Do not silently overwrite the source CV. When a complete tailored version is requested, create a clearly named new version.

A request tied to a specific vacancy may also use the Vacancy Fit workflow; the two capabilities can be combined.

Follow `../../references/evidence-rules.md` and `../../references/output-formats.md`.
