# Interview Review

Read the transcript or notes together with only the directly relevant career evidence.

Extract:
- confirmed candidate facts;
- useful examples, stories and metrics;
- corrections to existing information;
- recurring strengths or weaknesses in answers;
- wording worth reusing;
- preparation or evidence gaps;
- third-party feedback, kept explicitly attributed.

Do not convert interviewer or recruiter opinions into candidate facts.

Before adding transcript-derived durable facts to `profile.md`, distinguish what is directly confirmed by the candidate from what is only reported or inferred. If confirmation is needed and materially important, ask one focused question.

Update `summary.md` only when the interview changes current positioning, application status, open questions or next actions.

Follow `../../references/evidence-rules.md` and `../../references/output-formats.md`.
