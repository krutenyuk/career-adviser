# Vacancy Fit

Use the vacancy, relevant CV, `profile.md`, and any directly relevant interview or outcome material.

If the vacancy is supplied by link, preserve the source and save a faithful copy in the workspace when possible. If access is blocked, ask for the job description rather than guessing.

For each meaningful requirement classify the candidate as:
- Match;
- Partial match;
- No evidence;
- Gap.

Keep "No evidence" separate from "Gap". A capability may exist but not be demonstrated in the available material.

Assess:
- must-have requirements;
- important nice-to-haves;
- seniority and scope;
- domain/industry transferability;
- leadership expectations;
- location/work-authorisation constraints where relevant;
- terminology mismatch versus actual capability.

Give a realistic overall fit assessment. Do not inflate chances because the career path sounds plausible.

Recommend only material gap-closing actions and concrete CV positioning changes supported by evidence.

Ask a question only when the answer could materially change fit, positioning or application strategy.

Follow `../../references/evidence-rules.md` and `../../references/output-formats.md`.
